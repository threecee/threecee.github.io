# threecee apps

Personal iOS apps for Carl Christian Christensen and immediate family.

These apps are not on the App Store. Access is via Apple **TestFlight**
invite. If you've received an invite, follow [Install](/install).

## What's here

- **[HealthInsights](/healthinsights)** — visualizes patterns and
  insights from your HealthKit glucose, insulin, and carb data. iPhone
  only.
- **[MyLoop With Watch](/myloop)** — Carl's personal fork of the
  open-source Loop closed-loop insulin delivery app, with an Apple Watch
  companion that keeps Loop running when your iPhone is out of range.
  → For the novel watch behavior, see
  **[Watch dynamics](/myloop-watch-dynamics)**.
- **[Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting)** — what to do when something
  doesn't work as expected.
