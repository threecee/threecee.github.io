# Install

Both apps install via the same TestFlight workflow.

## You need

- An iPhone (iOS 16.2 or later).
- An invite email from Carl with the subject line "TestFlight Invitation".
- For MyLoop With Watch: an Apple Watch paired to your iPhone (watchOS
  10 or later).

## Steps

1. **Open the email** on your iPhone (not on a Mac or iPad).
2. **Tap the "View in TestFlight" button** in the email.
3. If you don't already have the **TestFlight** app, the App Store
   opens. Install TestFlight (it's free, by Apple), then return to the
   email and tap the link again.
4. In TestFlight, **tap "Accept"** for the app, then **"Install"**.
5. Wait for the download (~30–60 seconds).
6. Tap **"Open"** to launch the app.
7. When prompted, **grant HealthKit permissions** for the data the app
   needs.

## For MyLoop With Watch only

After the iPhone install completes, the watch app pushes to your paired
Apple Watch automatically. This takes 1–3 minutes.

To confirm: open the **Watch** app on your iPhone → scroll to
**"Available Apps"** or **"Installed on Apple Watch"** → look for
**MyLoop With Watch**. If it's listed under "Available", tap **Install**.

## A note on TestFlight invites

TestFlight invites expire after **90 days**. If your install link no
longer works, ask Carl to re-invite — the app you've already installed
keeps running in the meantime.
